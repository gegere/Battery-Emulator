## Capture workflow change

The v12.6 web interface replaces the old RAM CAN logger with **CAN tools** at
`/canreplay`. Its **Start dump** button opens the passive `/dump_can` stream.
The adjacent CAN replay controls transmit frames and are not part of recording.
On this LilyGo T-2CAN build, the old RAM `/export_can_log` endpoint is no longer
available; that export route now exists only for enabled SD logging.

Before relying on a new capture, verify that the chosen client saves the live
stream to a local file and that a short saved sample is readable by the offline
tools. Only one stream is supported: a second connection replaces the first.
Keep the connection open throughout the observation and retain its original
bytes. Start before payment/insertion and save through stop/removal. Label the
physical CAN segment and emulator uptime clock as in the existing test plan.

Streaming is not a guarantee of lossless capture. Its 8192-byte ring can emit
blank lines on overflow and can also drop frames without a marker. Preserve
blank lines and do not infer complete coverage from their absence. A continuous
hardware capture with explicit overrun counters remains preferable.
